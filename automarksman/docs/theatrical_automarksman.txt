THEATRICAL AUTOMARKSMAN 0.6.0 (TEST BUILD) - Kingdom Come: Deliverance II
by Flubbermunchkin

While Henry holds a drawn bow, the camera turns gently onto the head of the person (or the chest of the
animal) nearest the centre of your view, so that the arrow, not the middle of the screen, meets it.
People come first; your horse and dogs are left alone. The lock is soft: move the mouse and it lets
go; look away from the target and it is released, and the assist then picks whoever you are now
looking at. With ShowImpactMarker=1 a cross shows where an arrow loosed now would hit (green when it
is on the head bone, orange when the head could only be estimated) and a small white cross marks the
head. It allows for the arrow's drop, for air drag and for a target that is walking or running.

The arrow does not fly where the camera looks: the game launches it from the bow's own arrow joint,
along the bow's axis, which sits a couple of degrees off the view and moves as the bow sways. The mod
reads that live bow pose every frame (the same way the game's own trajectory preview does) and the
arrow speed the game's formula gives for your bow and arrow, and steers until that real path crosses
the head.

The camera stays calm: it only starts turning once the bow is actually drawn and steady (never while it
is being raised or the next arrow is nocked), it follows the bow's slow sway but not its tremor, and it
turns and stops smoothly. The marker is never smoothed: it always shows where an arrow loosed right now
would really go, so loose when it is green. If something looks wrong (the camera does not follow, the
bow pose cannot be read for a moment) the assist pauses and picks the aim up again instead of switching
itself off.

While the assist is on, the game's own aim sway (it slowly turns the view while you aim) is reduced to
SwayStrength percent of normal (25 by default, 0 = none, 100 = the game's own); switching the assist
off gives the game's sway back at once. A hand cannon is aimed through its fuse until the ball leaves.

Crossbows and firearms (hand cannons) go through the same game code as bows: the mod reads their live
launch pose, the game's speed formula and the projectile's own gravity and drag the same way, and only
steers once the weapon is actually aimed (never while it is being reloaded). Bows are tested in game;
crossbows and firearms are in this test build for the first time.

That is all it does. You still draw, hold and release the bow yourself. The game flies the arrow, sways
the bow with Henry's skill and stamina, and decides whether the arrow hits. Nothing is teleported, no
hit is forced, and no item, arrow or stat is changed.

Every arrow Henry looses is checked: the log compares its real launch with what the mod predicted, and
says how far above/below and left/right of the head it really passed. The first arrow also reads the
arrow's physics (gravity and air resistance). If the bow pose cannot be read, the mod falls back to the
camera plus offsets measured from earlier arrows (kcd2_automarksman_calibration.txt).

This is a test build.

REQUIREMENTS
  - Kingdom Come Script Extender (KCSE) by JerryYOJ
      https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3332
  - Address Library For KCSE by JerryYOJ (required by KCSE itself)
      https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance2/mods/3381

INSTALL
  Copy the "theatrical_automarksman" folder into <game>\Mods\
  (you should end up with <game>\Mods\theatrical_automarksman\KCSE\Plugins\kcd2_automarksman.dll).
  Uninstall: delete that folder.

HOW TO USE
  1. Press F4 to switch the assist on (a notice says "AutoMarksman on"). F4 again switches it off.
  2. Draw the bow and bring the target roughly to the middle of the screen (within about 10 degrees).
  3. The camera settles on the aim point; release when you like.
  Moving the mouse always wins: the assist lets go while you aim by hand and picks up again shortly after.

SETTINGS  (Mods\theatrical_automarksman\KCSE\Plugins\kcd2_automarksman.ini)
  [Keys]    Toggle=F4             on/off key
  [Options] StartEnabled=0        1 = on from the start
            ConeDegrees=10        how far from the centre of the view a target may be
            MaxDistance=80        metres
            TurnSpeed=115         fastest camera turn, degrees per second
            Responsiveness=9      how quickly the remaining error is closed
            HostileOnly=0         1 = people only while they are in combat
            TargetHorses=0, TargetDogs=0   horses and dogs (off: your own are never aimed at)
            ShowImpactMarker=1    draw the predicted impact and the head
            BowPose=1             aim with the bow's live pose (0 = camera plus measured offsets)
            SwayStrength=25       the game's own aim sway while the assist is on, % (0 = none, 100 = vanilla)
            SwayStrengthBow=-1, SwayStrengthCrossbow=-1, SwayStrengthGun=-1   per weapon (-1 = SwayStrength)
            SwaySmoothingMs=100   how closely the camera follows the bow's slow sway (higher = calmer)
            HoldWithinCm=2        the camera does not chase the last few centimetres
            SettleMs=200          steer only after the bow has been drawn and steady this long
            HeadBone=1            aim at the head bone (0 = estimate the head from the body's size)
            HeadAboveBoneCm=8     how far above the head bone to aim (the bone is at the base of the skull)
            HeadDropCm=13         without the bone: aim this far below the top of a person
            HandsOffMs=400        pause after you move the mouse
            ArrowSpeed=55         arrow speed guess (only without the bow pose)
            RememberBow=1         keep what was measured for the next session
            LeadMovingTargets=1   aim ahead of moving targets
            TargetPeople=1, TargetAnimals=1
            DebugLog=1            detailed log (kcd2_automarksman.log)

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - It does not check whether a wall or tree is between you and the target.
  - The aim assumes a full draw; an arrow loosed early is slower and drops more.
  - The game fires the arrow a moment after you release (on the animation), so a bow that is still
    swaying then can still move the arrow a little. A shaky, low-skill or tired Henry still misses.
  - Only tested statically against the Steam version 1.5.x, build 15693.

GAME UPDATES
  The mod does not use fixed memory addresses. At startup it finds the game code it needs by pattern
  and by class name. If a game update changes that code, the mod turns itself off and changes nothing.
