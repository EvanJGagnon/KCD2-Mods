THEATRICAL AUTOMARKSMAN 1.0.0 - Kingdom Come: Deliverance II
by Flubbermunchkin

While Henry aims a bow, a crossbow or a hand cannon, the camera turns gently onto the head of the person
(or the chest of the animal) nearest the centre of your view, so that the arrow, bolt or ball, not the
middle of the screen, meets it. F4 switches it on and off. You still draw, aim and release yourself.

People come first; your horse and dogs are left alone. Nobody behind a wall, terrain or other solid cover
is picked, and a shot whose arc would hit something on the way is not taken either. The lock is soft:
move the mouse and it lets go; look away from the target and it is released, and the assist then picks
whoever you are now looking at. With ShowImpactMarker=1 a cross shows where a shot loosed now would hit
(green when it is on the head bone, orange when the head could only be estimated) and a small white
cross marks the head. It allows for drop, air drag and for a target that is walking or running.

The arrow does not fly where the camera looks: the game launches it from the bow's own arrow joint, along
the bow's axis, which sits a couple of degrees off the view and moves as the bow sways. The mod reads that
live pose every frame (the same way the game's own trajectory preview does) and the speed the game's
formula gives for your bow and arrow, and steers until that real path crosses the head. A hand cannon is
aimed through its fuse until the ball actually leaves.

The camera stays calm: it only starts turning once the weapon is actually drawn and steady (never while it
is raised, reloaded or the next arrow is nocked), it follows the bow's slow sway but not its tremor, and
it turns and stops smoothly. While the assist is on, the game's own aim sway (it slowly turns the view
while you aim) is reduced to SwayStrength percent of normal (25 by default); switching the assist off
gives the game's sway back at once.

That is all it does. The game flies the arrow, sways the bow with Henry's skill and stamina, and decides
whether the shot hits. Nothing is teleported, no hit is forced, and no item, arrow or stat is changed.

CROSSBOWS
  A crossbow is aimed the same way, through its own live firing pose. Its animation moves more than a
  bow, so the camera follows only part of that motion (CrossbowSwayRateGain=40) and keeps the target
  until you move the mouse or lose sight of it. Set Crossbows=0 to leave crossbows completely to you.

INSTALL
  Vortex: install as usual.
  Manual: copy the "theatrical_automarksman" folder from this archive into <game>\Mods\
          (you should end up with <game>\Mods\theatrical_automarksman\KCSE\Plugins\kcd2_automarksman.dll).
  Uninstall: delete that folder.
  The ON/OFF messages (Localization folder) are only loaded if "theatrical_automarksman" is listed in
  <game>\Mods\mod_order.txt. Vortex takes care of that; with a manual install, add the line yourself if
  that file exists. Without it the mod still works, the messages just don't show properly.

HOW TO USE
  1. The assist is on from the start (StartEnabled=1); F4 switches it off and on again, with a message.
  2. Draw the bow (or raise the hand cannon) and bring the target roughly to the middle of the screen
     (within about 10 degrees).
  3. The camera settles on the aim point; release when you like.
  Moving the mouse always wins: the assist lets go while you aim by hand and picks up again shortly after.

SETTINGS  (Mods\theatrical_automarksman\KCSE\Plugins\kcd2_automarksman.ini)
  [Keys]    Toggle=F4             on/off key (F1-F12, a letter or digit, or a symbol key such as ])
  [Options] Notices=1             short ON/OFF message when you press the key
            Bows=1, HandCannons=1 which weapons are aimed for
            Crossbows=1           0 = crossbows left to you (camera and sway as vanilla)
            StartEnabled=1        1 = on from the start
            ConeDegrees=10        how far from the centre of the view a target may be
            MaxDistance=80        metres
            RequireClearShot=1    never aim at someone behind solid cover (0 = no check)
            TurnSpeed=115         fastest camera turn, degrees per second
            Responsiveness=9      how quickly the remaining error is closed
            HostileOnly=0         1 = people only while they are in combat
            TargetPeople=1, TargetAnimals=1, TargetHorses=0, TargetDogs=0
            ShowImpactMarker=1    draw the predicted impact and the head
            BowPose=1             aim with the weapon's live pose (0 = camera plus measured offsets)
            SwayStrength=25       the game's own aim sway while the assist is on, % (0 = none, 100 = vanilla)
            SwayStrengthBow=-1, SwayStrengthCrossbow=-1, SwayStrengthGun=-1   per weapon (-1 = SwayStrength)
            SwaySmoothingMs=100   how closely the camera follows the bow's slow sway (higher = calmer)
            CrossbowSwayRateGain=40   crossbows only: how much of the crossbow's own motion is followed, %
            HoldWithinCm=2        the camera does not chase the last few centimetres
            SettleMs=200          steer only after the weapon has been drawn and steady this long
            HeadBone=1            aim at the head bone (0 = estimate the head from the body's size)
            HeadAboveBoneCm=8     how far above the head bone to aim (the bone is at the base of the skull)
            HeadDropCm=13         without the bone: aim this far below the top of a person
            HandsOffMs=400        pause after you move the mouse
            ArrowSpeed=55         arrow speed guess (only without the bow pose)
            RememberBow=1         keep what was measured for the next session (kcd2_automarksman_calibration.txt)
            LeadMovingTargets=1   aim ahead of moving targets
            CrossbowPoseDiagnostics=0   detailed crossbow logging for bug reports (with DebugLog=1)
            DebugLog=0            1 = detailed log (kcd2_automarksman.log), including every shot's real miss

COMPATIBILITY
  - Works next to other KCSE plugins that hook the same game update (Horse Route Follow, Theatrical
    AutoSharpening): it starts a moment later and chains to them.
  - Played with Arrow Arc and Faster Bow Charge Duration installed. Mods that change bow, crossbow or
    arrow stats are fine: the mod reads the live weapon and the game's own speed formula.
  - Built and checked for the Steam version 1.5.x (build 15693). Other stores and versions are untested;
    on an unknown version the mod switches itself off and changes nothing.

KNOWN ISSUES AND LIMITATIONS
  - The aim assumes a full draw; an arrow loosed early is slower and drops more.
  - The game fires a moment after you release (on the animation), so a weapon that is still swaying
    then can still move the shot a little. A shaky, low-skill or tired Henry still misses.
  - Partial cover: the mod checks for a clear path to the head only. Someone behind a fence, railing,
    window bars or a low wall may be skipped even when part of them is visible, and a shot that would
    clip the top of the cover is not taken. Line of sight also ignores other people standing in the way.

GAME UPDATES
  The mod does not use fixed memory addresses. At startup it finds the game code it needs by pattern and
  by class name. If a game update changes that code, the mod turns itself off and changes nothing. The
  log then says "disabled: this game version is not supported yet".

BUG REPORTS
  Please include kcd2_automarksman.log (next to the DLL, rewritten every session), with DebugLog=1.
